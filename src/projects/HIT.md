# HIT Project

H.I.T. (Handicap Innovation Territoire) is a collaborative project supported by Lorient Agglomération, with the ambition of turning disability into a lever for social and technological innovation in the service of citizens, while promoting the region's economic attractiveness. IMT Atlantique oversees protecting the privacy of people with disabilities returning to their own homes in an assisted living context. The technologies we are proposing aims at optimizing the subset of features needed to perform medical surveillance while preserving PII of the patients.
